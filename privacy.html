Privacy statement

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Who we are

Spaakwerk Fietsenmakers, Noorderstraat 12, 1031 AB Amsterdam. KvK 12345678. You can reach us at info@spaakwerk.example. We decide what happens with the data described on this page.

What this website does

It loads its own pages, fonts and images from one place. There are no analytics, no ads, no social media widgets and no tracking cookies. There is no form on the site, so the site itself does not ask you for personal data.

Hosting

Our hosting provider delivers the website to you. Like most hosts, it may keep technical logs, such as your IP address and the time of your visit, to keep the site running and safe. We do not use these logs ourselves.

When you book on WhatsApp

The “Book on WhatsApp” buttons open WhatsApp with a ready-made message. If you send it, we receive your phone number, your name if your profile shows it, and what you write. WhatsApp is run by Meta and has its own privacy policy.

We use these details to plan your repair, to tell you the price and to tell you when your bike is ready. Our legal basis is taking steps at your request before a repair agreement (and the agreement itself). We keep the chat only as long as we need it for that, and for questions about your repair afterwards. We do not share it with anyone else.

Links to other sites

The map, WhatsApp and Instagram links go to other websites. They only get your data if you click them.

Cookies and browser storage

We set no cookies and store nothing in your browser. See our cookie policy.

Your rights

You can ask us to show, correct or delete the data we hold about you, to limit its use, or to object to it. Email info@spaakwerk.example. We answer within one month. You can also complain to the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).
